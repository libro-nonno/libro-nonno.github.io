My deepest thanks go to all the people mentioned in this book, who – directly or indirectly, willingly or not – played a part in the events I have recounted. Without them, many of these memories would never have come to life.

A special thanks goes to my wife Piera. This year, 2025, we celebrated our fiftieth wedding anniversary: an important milestone, made up of highs and lows and many rewards. With her I have shared the most beautiful and meaningful memories of these fifty years: our wedding, our children and their academic and professional achievements, and the grandchildren who fill our lives with joy.
Memories far too precious for these pages, yet engraved in my heart.

I owe to her the enthusiasm and the strength to write down these recollections, to look back on the past with joy and a touch of irony. You can only do that when you are serene, at peace with yourself and with those you love. To Piera go all the credit and my profound gratitude for helping me become the person I am today.

My thanks also go to my daughter Linda, my son Filippo, and my grandchildren Camilla and Giacomo, who supported me with the technical, digital, and graphic aspects of this work.

Pierluigi
