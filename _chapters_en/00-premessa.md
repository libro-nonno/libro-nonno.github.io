In the end, the desire to put some of my memories down on paper won over my natural reluctance to talk about myself. What follows are scattered fragments of my childhood, my adolescence, and later years—episodes that kept knocking on the door of my memory like old friends eager to be heard once again.

Don’t expect a biography, nor a diary. This is, rather, a small album of stories. Some I lived first-hand; others were recounted so many times by my siblings or relatives that they eventually became mine as well.
I told myself: “It’s now or never.” Because it would be a shame to let these stories slip into oblivion. And so here I am, pen in hand—or better, fingers on the keyboard—chasing images of the past, trying to follow a more or less chronological order of events.

I believe I’ll enjoy writing these stories as much as, I hope, you’ll enjoy reading them. After all, every story is a little journey through time.
